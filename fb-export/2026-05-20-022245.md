Also, distance between inner glass and paper will matter. In beginning, when roll is new, the distance between paper and inner glass in less. After 100 votes or more votes, distance between paper and glass will be more and paper will be less visible. 
.
And light level in booth matters. Many trainning video are shot before camera and camera men use very bight light for shooting. In very bright light,  paper behind glass will be visible. But light level inside booth, near voting area is low.
.
And all glasses need not be equally dark.
.
And finally, we have LightSensorLogic. If voter is standing in front of vvpat, vvpat lightsensor will register low intensity. Say voter presses Banana. if voter leaves before printing has started, then vvpat can just turn light ON, show nothing, turn light OFF, and print anything. Or, say voter stood for 4 seconds, but left before 10 seconds, then after turning light OFF, printer can scroll paper back inside. Now if next voter presses Banana, then vvpat can show same print. And if voter presses Apple or WaterMelon , then witn light OFF, existing print can be disposed off and new Apple or WaterMelon can be printed. 
.
So we have several factors
.
(1) how black is glass - are all glasses equally black? In 2019 and now?

(2) distance between paper and glass - after 100 or more votes, distance between paper and glass is higher which decreases visibility 

(3) light intensity in actual booth and light intensity in trainning video - light intensity in trainning video shooting is much higher and so paper may become visible in trainning videos

(4) lightsensor tells vvpat if voter is present or has left 

(5) whether printer has reverse gear or not
