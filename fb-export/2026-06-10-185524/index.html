#VoteVapsi_Education_Minister Law

In the current system, the Education Minister publishes such laws that have led to a continuous decline in the standards of mathematics and science education in Bharat and have weakened the infrastructure of government schools. As a result, people are often compelled to rely on expensive private schools, making education increasingly costly and difficult to afford. Several laws introduced by the Education Minister have not only made education more expensive but have also encouraged malpractices , bribery / corruption in various high-level competitive examinations. Under the proposed VoteVapsi Education Minister Law, if the Education Minister implements policies or laws that weakens education system, then voters of the state will be able to replace the minister anytime before their term through a VoteVapsi Passbook mechanism.

The main points for the procedure of replacing the Education Minister are given below :

  1. If any citizen above 30 years of age wants to become Education Minister, he can present an affidavit in front of the collector. The Collector will declare him as the Education Minister candidate by taking a fee of ₹ 10,000 and will make the affidavit public on the Chief Minister's website.

  2. With the publication of this law, every Voter shall get a VoteVapsi Passbook. If you are not satisfied with the work of the Education Minister and want to replace him, then you can register your Approval Yes or No(opinion like Votes) in Patwari's (Talati) office. You can register your Approval (yes or no) via SMS also. You can give your approval or cancel it any day, this approval is not your vote but rather a suggestion.

  3. If a candidate for the post of health minister gets 25% approval of total voters from the state (not just those who registered their approvals), and if these approvals exceed 1% approvals of the incumbent Education Minister, then Chief Minister may or may not appoint the person who gets the most approval by removing the current Education Minister.

[Note: If the person getting the most approvals is not a member of the current Legislative Assembly / Legislative Council, then such a person can become an MLA by contesting the election of MLA in any election or by-election to be held in the next 6 months after becoming a Minister.]

For implementing this law, there is no need for any constitutional amendment or passing it by the parliament/assembly. The Prime Minister can implement it in all the states by directly publishing it in the Gazette. If the Chief Minister wants, he can implement it in his state through Gazette Notification as well. After publishing this law, there will be a healthy competition among the MLAs to become Education Minister & they will start coming before Citizens with proposals of improving government schools, college, Maths / Science education, making quality education affordable to all, etc. so that the Citizens can give them approvals to become Education Minister.
