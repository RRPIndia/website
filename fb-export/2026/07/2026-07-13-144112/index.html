STRONGLY disagree with 20-jul march towards Parliament - 3 solutions I propose are - andolans / electioning for (a) right to recall over ministers to increase accountability (b) usa like 2 year Loksabha / Vidhansabha terms to increase accountability (c) ballot paper option instead of EVM alone
.
(1) First, I strongly disagree with march towards Parliament. Parliament belongs to PEOPLE of Bharat - it is not some palace of king. Lack of "accountability" is because lack of right to recall on Education Ministers and other ministers , 5 year loksabha term instead of usa like 2 year term and absence of paper ballots.
.
(2) So using andolan and election contesting, we can correct all these flaws. Using andolans and election contesting, we can force MPs to bring referendum law. Or, without referendum law , using andolan and electioneering ,we can force MPs to amend People Prepresentation Act to have 2 year Loksabha Vidhansabha terms. And using andolan + electioneering , we can bring RTR Education Minister and other Ministers. For ballot papers, andolan alone is way. THERE IS NO NEED TO MARCH TOWARDS PARLIAMENT.

(3) But again, CJP apex did NOT even support paper ballot demand which is 10s of time more imp than resignation of a 2 bit minister resignation. Over 90% CJP supporters aka HoneyBees , support ballot paper. And since day-one, lakhs of HoneyBees (aka CJP supporters) have been asking CJP-apex to make ballot paper as one imp issue. But CJP-apex has been using "next life time" sentences to kill the ballot paper issue. Same way , ArKe and Anna opposed ballot paper in year 2011.

(4) over 90% HoneyBees aka CJP supporters also support usa like 2 year LS VS terms and want to make it andolan point. But again CJP-apex is killing this proposal using "next life time" sentences,

(5) So Imo, we must continue andolan for accountability, by demanding usa like 2 year terms for LS VS , RTR over all Ministers and ballot papers. But Parliament is NOT a King Palace. And march towards Parliament event MUST BE CANCELED.
