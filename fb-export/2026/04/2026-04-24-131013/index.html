Each voter gives votes to 4 candidates. Now amongst 4 corporators - 1 is general, 1 is general women, 1 is SC ST or OBC and 1 is SC ST or OBC women. So there is 50% reservation for women, and 50% reservation for SC ST OBC. Also, all 10 Municipal corporations have same system - each ward has 4 corporators.
